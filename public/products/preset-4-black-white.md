# Black & White Classic Preset

Timeless elegance with our Black & White Classic preset. Perfect tonal range and contrast make this ideal for any black and white photography style.

## Features

- **Perfect Tonal Range**: From pure blacks to bright whites
- **Contrast Optimization**: Professional-grade contrast
- **Grain Control**: Optional film grain effect
- **Shadow Detail**: Preserves shadow information
- **Highlight Recovery**: Recovers blown highlights

## Perfect For

- Portrait photography
- Documentary photography
- Fine art photography
- Wedding photography
- Architectural photography

## Installation

1. Download the preset file
2. Open Lightroom Classic
3. Go to Develop Module
4. Right-click on Presets panel
5. Select "Import Presets"
6. Choose the downloaded file
7. Start using immediately

## Compatibility

Works with Lightroom Classic 2015 and newer versions. Also compatible with Lightroom CC.
