# Warm Tones Preset

Transform your photography with our professional Warm Tones preset. Perfect for portrait photography and golden hour shots, this preset enhances warm colors while maintaining natural skin tones.

## Features

- **Golden Hour Enhancement**: Brings out the best in golden hour lighting
- **Skin Tone Optimization**: Carefully calibrated for natural-looking skin tones
- **Warm Color Grading**: Adds warmth without oversaturation
- **One-Click Application**: Simply apply and adjust to your needs

## Perfect For

- Portrait photography
- Wedding photography
- Golden hour sessions
- Lifestyle photography
- Travel photography

## Installation

1. Download the preset file
2. Open Lightroom Classic
3. Go to Develop Module
4. Right-click on Presets panel
5. Select "Import Presets"
6. Choose the downloaded file
7. Start using immediately

## Compatibility

Works with Lightroom Classic 2015 and newer versions. Also compatible with Lightroom CC.
